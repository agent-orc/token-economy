Quality Studio history is observational operational evidence, not a controlled A/B comparison. A finding confirmation rate is a precision signal over sighted findings; it does not measure missed findings or recall. Fixture runs validate only the ingestion contract and never contribute to model metrics. Price and quota may rank equivalent available routes or contribute only the declared 0-5 intake points. They never lower a hard correctness floor. Unknown prices, missing trust denominators, unverified media costs, unsupported model/level pairs, restrictions, deprecations, and provisional evidence remain explicit.

Trust reconciliation: named models are `unverifiedNoDenominator`; recovered historical incidents remain attributed to `unattributed`. Neither state is converted to a zero violation rate or provider ranking.

## Reissue rule

Semantic reissues and substantive C/D reviews set empirical confidence to `10` and raise the next attempt by at least `1` core tier. The do-not-promote outcomes are `environmentalFailure`, `staleBase`, `brokenTestHost`, `cancellation`, `quotaTruncation`, `missingDeliveryPath`. After `2` semantic failures at the stronger tier, escalation stops for task narrowing, better evidence, or a human decision.
